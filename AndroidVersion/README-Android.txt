THE LAST JOURNEY — Android Edition (v2.2.7)
============================================

WHAT'S NEW IN v2.2.7
---------------------
- PAUSE IS A MENU. ESC or P used to freeze the game under one line of text,
  and the only thing on offer was to begin the journey again. The pause
  screen is a window now: RESUME, RESTART THIS ROAD, SOUND, QUIT TO THE
  TITLE. Choose with W/S or the arrows, take a row with E, step back out
  with ESC. R still restarts the road outright.
- RESTART THIS ROAD. A fight that had gone wrong on the dragon's bridge
  cost the whole stage, so the only way out was to replay it from the first
  cliff. The menu puts her back at the first step of the road she is
  standing on, her breath whole, and keeps everything the road cannot take:
  her souls, her elements, her guardian, her ward. The peddler's stall is
  waiting at the start of it, as it is at the start of every road. The last
  street restarts the fight itself, the way dying there always did.
- QUIT TO THE TITLE. Leaving a run no longer means reloading the page. The
  journey is let go whole — souls, powers, wares, the voyage — and the
  title comes back over the first road.
- THE SOUND ROW SHOWS WHICH WAY IT IS, and on a phone the buttons already
  on screen drive all of it: the arrows choose, E takes the row, and the
  restart button beside the music note restarts the road.

WHAT'S NEW IN v2.2.6
---------------------
- HER X IS NEVER IDLE. On a stretch of road with nothing on it, X used to do
  nothing at all: the volley needed something to aim at before it would
  leave her hand. She throws on an empty road now, and the shards fly the
  way she faces until they burn themselves out. The moment anything is in
  reach they turn and find it, exactly as before.
- THE WARD CAN BE BOUGHT BACK. Once the shield bar was broken the stall
  showed the ward as OWNED for the rest of the run, however many souls she
  carried. It is sold like her health now: while the bar is whole the stall
  marks it WHOLE, and once it has been spent 50 souls fills it to the brim
  again. The road still never mends it by itself.

WHAT'S NEW IN v2.2.5
---------------------
- HER X WEARS THE MIX TOO. With two or three elements bought, her X threw
  the plain fireball whatever else was in it, so HOT MIST, PLASMA and STORM
  all looked like fire. Fire and ice now throw a scalding cloud with the
  ember still glowing inside it. Fire and the charge throw the violet bolt
  sheathed in flame. Ice and the charge throw cold lightning with a crystal
  riding on it. All three throw the flame tail, the violet charge and the
  cold white head at once. One element on its own looks exactly as before.

WHAT'S NEW IN v2.2.4
---------------------
- THE LAST FIGHT CAN BE RETRIED. Dying on the last street left her with no
  breath at all, so the retry began at nothing: her X threw one volley and
  the street went dark again. A retry now gives her life back whole.
- THE WARD IS SPENT, NOT MENDED. Her shield bar no longer fills itself at
  the start of every stage. Whatever is left of it is what the next road
  starts with, and only the peddler's stall fills it again.
- HER X WEARS HER ELEMENTS. Fire throws a small fireball with its tail
  behind it and roars. Ice throws the same white shard she started with,
  and breaks like glass. Electric throws a violet bolt with a hard snap.
- THE LAST BRIDGE IS ONLY A BRIDGE. The wells that sat at the first and
  last stretch of the planks are gone: cliff, one cut, one deck across it.
  The dragon keeps to the planks, leaps end to end, and lands on the boards.
- THE DRAGON SEES HER SOONER. The fight starts on the approach to the
  bridge instead of when she is already standing inside its chest.

WHAT'S NEW IN v2.2.3

IMPORTANT — ONE-TIME UNINSTALL
------------------------------
v1.0–v1.3 were signed with a debug key, and Android only installs an update
when the signature matches. Installing v1.4 over v1.3 therefore says "App
not installed". Uninstall the old copy once, install v1.4, and from then on
every update installs in place.

INSTALL (sideload, ~2 minutes)
------------------------------
0. Coming from v1.3 or older: uninstall it first (one time only, see
   above). Coming from v1.4 or later: just install, it updates in place.
1. Copy TheLastJourney.apk onto your Android phone or tablet
   (USB, Bluetooth, cloud drive, or download it directly).
2. Tap the APK file. If asked, allow "Install from unknown sources"
   for the app you used to open it (Files / Chrome).
3. Tap Install. That's it — "The Last Journey" appears in your app
   drawer with its own icon.

REQUIREMENTS
------------
- Android 5.0 (Lollipop) or newer (ARM & x86, phones and tablets).
- Targets Android 17 (API 37), so it runs cleanly on current phones.
- No internet permission, no ads, fully offline, ~95 KB.
- Android 17 ignores orientation locks on large screens, so on tablets
  and in desktop mode the window may be any shape; the game scales to fit.

BUILDING A NEW APK (scripted)
-----------------------------
build-apk.sh does the whole release in one command:

  cd AndroidVersion
  ./build-apk.sh                 # keep the version name, versionCode + 1
  ./build-apk.sh -n 1.5 -c 7     # set the version explicitly

It bundles src/ with esbuild, rebuilds the APK with apktool, aligns it,
signs it with the release keystore, checks the certificate and writes
AndroidVersion/TheLastJourney.apk. Use -o <file> to write the result
somewhere else, or -t <file> to build from a different base APK.

It needs JDK 17+, Android build-tools with apksigner and zipalign (37.x),
apktool, and Node with esbuild. If they are not on PATH, point at them:

  APKTOOL_JAR=/path/to/apktool.jar \
  ANDROID_HOME=/path/to/android-sdk \
  ESBUILD=/path/to/esbuild \
  ./build-apk.sh

Files:
  signing/thelastjourney-release.jks   release keystore (alias: lastjourney)
  signing/keystore.properties          its passwords, read by the script

Keep both and never regenerate the keystore: a new key means a new
signature, and everyone would have to uninstall again. Keep this repository
private, and keep a backup of the .jks somewhere safe. The script refuses
to finish if the signing certificate is not the expected one:
  32:7F:31:2B:E0:DE:A7:CB:80:50:99:17:31:24:42:BD:26:DE:BF:AE:99:13:02:32:73:C0:DC:72:EB:EE:6E:F6

This build: versionName 1.6, versionCode 7, package com.zai.lastjourney.

HOW TO PLAY ON TOUCH
--------------------
The game auto-scales to your screen in landscape. On-screen controls:

  Bottom-left:   <  >   walk left / right
  Bottom-right:  ^      jump (hold in mid-air to GLIDE once learned)
                 E      interact / talk / collect / continue
                 >>     dash         (appears once unlocked)
                 *      white shards (appears once unlocked)
                 ~      light beam   (hold; appears once unlocked)
  Top-right:     ||     pause        (RESTART button appears while paused)
                 note   mute / unmute

- Title screen: tap anywhere to begin.
- Text notes: tap E to skip typing, tap E again to close.
- Hardware BACK button = pause. Press BACK twice quickly to exit.

TIP: a keyboard also works if you plug one in (arrows/WASD, E, J, X, C,
P, M, R) — the desktop layout is untouched.

TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------
- "App not installed" coming from v1.3 or older: that is the signature
  change — uninstall the old copy once, then install again. From v1.4 on,
  updates install over the app without uninstalling.
- Still a black screen? Make sure you uninstalled the old copy first, then
  install again. If "Could not start the game engine..." appears, update
  "Android System WebView" in the Play Store and relaunch.
- No sound on first launch: tap once anywhere; Android requires one
  interaction before audio can start.
- If controls feel small on a tablet, they are always in the same
  corners — muscle memory kicks in fast.
